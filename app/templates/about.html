About the System

AI-Powered Livestock Health Management for Modern Farming

Machine Learning–Based Diagnostic System for Common Sheep Diseases with Integrated Health Status Monitoring

The Sheep Disease Detection System is an AI-powered web application that helps identify common sheep diseases through image analysis. This project was developed as an undergraduate Computer Engineering thesis project at the University of Rizal System - Morong, combining artificial intelligence, computer vision, and health monitoring technologies to create a comprehensive solution for livestock health management.

Technology Stack

  • Model: MobileNetV2 (Transfer Learning)
  • Training Method: Stratified Split Validation
  • Framework: TensorFlow/Keras
  • Backend: Flask (Python)
  • Frontend: Bootstrap 5
  • Database: SQLite
  • Training Images: 733 images (4 classes)
  • Test Images: 147 images (stratified split)

Key Features

  • AI Disease Detection: Identifies 4 common sheep diseases with high accuracy
  • Health Monitoring: Real-time tracking of vital signs and environmental conditions
  • Overall Health Index (OHI): Comprehensive health scoring system
  • Data Export: CSV and PDF reports for record-keeping
  • Prediction History: Track all disease detections over time
  • Responsive Design: Works on desktop, tablet, and mobile devices

How It Works

  1. Image Upload: User uploads a clear photo of the sheep
  2. Preprocessing: Image is resized and normalized for the model
  3. AI Analysis: MobileNetV2 model processes the image
  4. Disease Detection: Model predicts the most likely disease with confidence score
  5. Results Display: User receives diagnosis with disease information and recommendations
  6. Health Monitoring: Optional integration with sensors for continuous health tracking

Detectable Diseases

Flystrike

Parasitic condition caused by fly larvae infestation

Healthy

No disease detected, sheep in good health

Orf

Viral infection causing sores around mouth and lips

Sheep Scab

Parasitic skin disease caused by mites
